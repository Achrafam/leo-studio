What Leo Studio is

Leo Studio is a private tool that creates short, original animated stories and sends them to its creator's own TikTok drafts using TikTok's official Content Posting API. The creator reviews every draft and decides whether to publish it. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Who may use it

Only the creator who runs Leo Studio, with their own TikTok account. Using it requires accepting TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, which always take precedence.

Content

  • All characters, scripts, animation and narration are original and made by the tool. Music comes from royalty-free libraries used under their licences.
  • The tool never copies or re-uploads other creators' videos, sounds or characters.
  • Videos are labelled as AI-generated content when they are published, as TikTok requires.
  • Nothing is posted publicly by the tool. Publishing is always a manual action by the creator in the TikTok app.

Acceptable use

Leo Studio must not be used for spam, misleading content, impersonation, artificial engagement or anything that breaks TikTok's rules. It respects TikTok's API limits, including the limit on pending drafts.

The tool is provided as is, without any guarantee of availability, reach, views or income. The creator is responsible for what they choose to publish.

Ending use

Access can be stopped at any time by revoking Leo Studio in TikTok's app permissions settings. These terms may be updated; the effective date above will change when they are.
